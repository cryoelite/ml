The `arg` prefix is a convention worth learning once: `argmax` gives you *where*
the maximum is rather than what it is, and `argsort` gives you the ordering
rather than the ordered values.

That indirection is the point. Once you have the order, you can apply it to any
number of parallel arrays — sort your predictions and carry the labels, the IDs
and the timestamps along with them.

Reverse with `[::-1]` for descending; NumPy has no `reverse=True`.

For a top-k on a large array, `np.argpartition` is much faster because it only
partially sorts. Retrieving the 10 nearest neighbours out of a million vectors is
`argpartition`, not `argsort`, and the difference is real.
