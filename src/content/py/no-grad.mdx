PyTorch normally records operations on tensors that require gradients. This record is needed for backpropagation and uses memory.

Use torch.no_grad() or torch.inference_mode() for prediction when gradients are not needed. model.eval() is separate: it changes the behavior of layers such as dropout and batch normalisation. Evaluation code often needs both.
