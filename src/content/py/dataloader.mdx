The separation is the good idea: `Dataset` knows how to read and decode one
example, and knows nothing about batching; `DataLoader` knows about batching
and shuffling, and nothing about your file format. Swap either independently.

`num_workers` spawns processes that prefetch while the GPU is busy. On a small
in-memory dataset it costs more than it saves; on a directory of JPEGs it is
the difference between a GPU at 95% utilisation and one at 20%.
