  targ = torch.randn(8)         # what your labels look like
  (pred - targ).shape, (pred.squeeze() - targ).shape
output: '(torch.Size([8, 8]), torch.Size([8]))'

Unsqueeze adds a dimension of size one; squeeze removes one or more such dimensions. Use a specific dimension with squeeze when you need to avoid removing the batch dimension too.

A common shape bug occurs when predictions have shape (n, 1) and targets have shape (n,). Broadcasting can create an (n, n) result instead of comparing matching values. Check prediction and target shapes before calculating loss.
