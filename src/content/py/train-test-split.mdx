Always pass `random_state` so the split is reproducible, and pass
`stratify=y` for classification so that rare classes appear in both halves in
the same proportion. Without stratification, a class making up 2% of the data
can end up entirely inside the training set, and your validation score becomes
a measurement of a question you never asked.

A random split is wrong whenever rows are not independent — time series,
multiple photos of the same patient, repeated users. See
[Generalisation](/learn/06-generalisation/) for what to do instead.
