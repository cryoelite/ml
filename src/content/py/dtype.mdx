A dtype describes the kind of values an array stores, such as integers or floating-point numbers. Integer arrays cannot represent fractions, so assigning a fractional value may truncate it.

Choose the dtype deliberately when precision matters. Check it alongside shape when a calculation gives unexpected results. PyTorch tensors also need compatible dtypes for many operations.
