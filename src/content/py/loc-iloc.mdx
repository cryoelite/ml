title: 'df.loc / df.iloc'
lib: 'pandas'
summary: "Select pandas rows by label or integer position."
signature: 'df.loc[row_label, col_label]   |   df.iloc[row_pos, col_pos]'
example: |-
  df.loc[df['age'] > 30, ['city']]
  df.iloc[0:2, 0]
rust: "iloc selects by integer position. loc selects by labels or conditions."
docs: 'https://pandas.pydata.org/docs/user_guide/indexing.html'
docsLabel: 'Indexing guide ↗'
---
In pandas, loc selects rows and columns by labels. iloc selects by integer position.

A loc slice includes its end label; an iloc slice excludes its stop position, like a normal Python slice. Use a boolean mask with loc to filter rows.
