Columnar storage is the whole point. Computing the mean of one column touches
one contiguous array rather than striding across a million row structs, and
each column can have its own dtype without boxing.

`df.head()`, `df.describe()` and `df.dtypes` are the first three things to run
on any dataset you have not seen before, in that order.
