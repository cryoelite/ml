The magic that is worth knowing about: assigning a `Parameter` or a submodule
to `self` registers it. That is how `net.parameters()` can enumerate every
weight in a hundred-layer network without you maintaining a list, and how
`net.to('mps')` can move all of them at once.

Call the module, do not call `forward` directly. `net(x)` runs hooks that
`net.forward(x)` skips, and some of them matter.
