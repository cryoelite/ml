Meshgrid turns two one-dimensional coordinate arrays into two grids containing every coordinate pair. Applying a function to those grids evaluates it over a two-dimensional area.

It is often used to draw contours and decision boundaries. The indexing option controls how the two input axes map to rows and columns.
