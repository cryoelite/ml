  opt = torch.optim.Adam(model.parameters(), lr=1e-3)
  opt.zero_grad(); loss.backward(); opt.step()
example: 'opt = torch.optim.SGD(net.parameters(), lr=0.1, momentum=0.9)'
rust: 'A struct owning `&mut` references to the weights plus whatever per-parameter state the rule needs (a momentum buffer, running averages). `step()` is the mutation.'

Those three lines in that order are the training loop. Every variation you
will ever read — gradient clipping, accumulation, mixed precision, schedulers
— is an insertion into that sequence, not a replacement for it.

Default to `Adam` (or `AdamW`) when you do not want to think, and `SGD` with
momentum when you are chasing the last percent on a vision model.
