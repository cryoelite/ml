A classifier does not output a class; it outputs a score per class. `argmax`
along the class axis is the step that turns "0.1, 0.7, 0.2" into "class 1".

Note that this throws away the model's confidence. `0.34, 0.33, 0.33` and
`0.98, 0.01, 0.01` both argmax to 0, and the difference between them is
usually the most useful thing the model told you.
