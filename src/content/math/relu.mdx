It looks too simple to matter, and it is the single most consequential
practical detail in the move to deep networks.

Its derivative is 1 for positive inputs and 0 for negative — no saturation, no
shrinking factor, so gradients pass through many layers intact. Compare
sigmoid's maximum derivative of 0.25 and the difference over thirty layers is
the difference between training and not training.

It is also almost free to compute: a comparison and a select, against an
exponential.

The cost is dead units — a neuron whose input is always negative outputs zero
forever and receives no gradient to recover with. Leaky ReLU, GELU and SiLU
are the usual patches, and GELU is what transformers actually use.
