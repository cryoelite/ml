The rectified linear unit (ReLU) returns zero for negative inputs and returns positive inputs unchanged:

$$\mathrm{ReLU}(z)=\max(0,z)$$

Its derivative is one for positive inputs and zero for negative inputs. This simple activation helped make it easier to train many-layer networks than with saturating activations such as sigmoid.

A unit that remains negative receives no gradient through ReLU and may stop updating. Leaky ReLU, GELU, and SiLU are alternatives with different behavior.
