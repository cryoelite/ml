A logit is an unbounded score. In binary classification, the sigmoid maps it to a probability. A logit of zero corresponds to probability 0.5; a positive logit gives a probability above 0.5.

During training, pass logits to a loss designed for logits, such as PyTorch’s binary cross-entropy with logits or multiclass cross-entropy. These losses handle the probability conversion in a numerically stable way.

Convert logits to probabilities when you need probabilities for evaluation, thresholds, or display.
