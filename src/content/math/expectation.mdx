Expectation means a probability-weighted average. For a dataset, the average of a quantity across examples estimates its expected value under the data distribution.

For example, minimising $\mathbb{E}_{(x,y)\sim D}[L(f_\theta(x),y)]$ means choosing parameters that make the average prediction loss small.

When you read $\mathbb{E}$ in a paper, read it as “average over.”
