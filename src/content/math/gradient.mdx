If the loss is a landscape and the parameters are your coordinates, the
gradient is the compass bearing pointing straight uphill. Training walks the
other way.

The gradient has exactly as many components as the model has parameters — for a
large language model, hundreds of billions of numbers, recomputed for every
batch. That this is affordable at all is the achievement of
[backpropagation](/learn/09-backpropagation/), which computes the whole vector
in roughly the cost of one extra forward pass.
