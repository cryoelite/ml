The gradient is the vector of partial derivatives of a scalar function. It has one component for each input or model parameter.

The gradient points in the direction of steepest local increase. Gradient-based training usually moves in the opposite direction to reduce the loss.

Backpropagation computes gradients for all model parameters efficiently by applying the chain rule through the network.
