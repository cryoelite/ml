Mean squared error (MSE) is the average squared difference between predictions and targets:

$$\mathrm{MSE} = \frac{1}{n}\sum_i (\hat{y}_i-y_i)^2$$

Squaring prevents positive and negative errors from cancelling. It also gives large errors more weight: an error of 10 contributes 100 times as much as an error of 1.

MSE is useful for many regression tasks, but it is sensitive to outliers. Mean absolute error or Huber loss may be more suitable when large deviations should have less influence.
