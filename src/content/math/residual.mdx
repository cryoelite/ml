A residual connection adds a block’s input to its output:

$$y = x + f(x)$$

The block learns an adjustment $f(x)$ while the original input can pass through unchanged. This can help information and gradients move through deep networks.

Residual connections are used in ResNets and many transformer architectures. They help with optimisation, but they do not guarantee that a network will train successfully.
