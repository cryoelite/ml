A dot product multiplies corresponding values in two vectors and adds the results. It is positive when the vectors point in similar directions, zero when they are perpendicular, and negative when they point in opposing directions.

A linear model uses a dot product of weights and features. Attention compares queries with keys the same way. Cosine similarity divides a dot product by the lengths of both vectors, so it measures direction without their scale.
