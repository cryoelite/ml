---

The second form is the one to remember: a dot product is large and positive
when two vectors point the same way, zero when they are perpendicular, and
negative when they oppose.

That is why it appears everywhere. A linear model's prediction is the dot
product of weights and features — "how much does this example agree with what
I have learned to look for". Cosine similarity between embeddings is a dot
product with the lengths divided out. Attention scores in a transformer are
dot products between queries and keys. Same operation, three names.
