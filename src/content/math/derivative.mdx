Everything in training reduces to this question: *if I increase this number a
little, does the error go up or down, and by how much?* The derivative is the
answer, and a negative derivative means "increase this to reduce the error".

You will never compute a limit by hand here. What matters is the intuition —
slope — and the three rules that get composed millions of times a second:
the power rule, the product rule, and above all
[the chain rule](/appendix/math/#chain-rule).
