A derivative measures how much a function’s output changes when its input changes by a small amount. It is the slope of the function at a point.

During training, derivatives tell us how a small change to a parameter affects the loss. A negative derivative means increasing that parameter would locally reduce the loss.

You will not need to calculate limits by hand here. The power rule, product rule, and [chain rule](/appendix/math/#chain-rule) are enough for the examples in this course.
