Softmax converts a vector of class scores into positive values that sum to one:

$$\mathrm{softmax}(z)_i = \frac{e^{z_i}}{\sum_j e^{z_j}}$$

Larger scores receive larger probabilities. Dividing the scores by a temperature before applying softmax can make the distribution flatter or sharper.

For numerical stability, implementations subtract the largest score before exponentiating. Classification losses often take logits directly and apply this calculation internally.
