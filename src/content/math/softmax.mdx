Sigmoid generalised from two classes to many. Exponentiating makes everything
positive; dividing by the total makes it sum to one.

The exponential is not decoration — it makes the function *soft-max* rather
than *mean*. Scores of $[1, 2, 3]$ become roughly $[0.09, 0.24, 0.67]$: the
largest score takes a share well beyond its arithmetic weight. Divide the
scores by a temperature first and you control how sharp that is, which is
exactly the `temperature` parameter you have seen on a language-model API.

Implemented naively it overflows. Every real implementation subtracts the
maximum score first — see [np.exp](/appendix/python/#exp-log).
