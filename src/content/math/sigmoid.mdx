The sigmoid function maps any real number to a value between zero and one:

$$\sigma(z)=\frac{1}{1+e^{-z}}$$

For binary classification, the output can represent the model’s probability of one class. A logit of zero maps to 0.5; very positive or negative logits map close to one or zero.

The derivative becomes small for saturated inputs. This can slow learning when sigmoid is used in many hidden layers, so modern networks often use other activations there.
