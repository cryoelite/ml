Layer normalisation rescales the features within one example. It uses that example’s mean and variance, then applies learned scale and shift parameters.

Batch normalisation instead computes statistics across examples in a batch. Layer normalisation therefore does not make one example’s output depend on which other examples share its batch. This is useful for variable-length sequences and batch sizes that change between training and prediction.

The parameters $\gamma$ and $\beta$ let the model adjust the normalised values. Transformers commonly use layer normalisation or a related variant.
