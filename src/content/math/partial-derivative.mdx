A model has millions of parameters, and the loss is one number that depends on
all of them. The partial derivative with respect to a single weight asks:
holding all the others where they are, does nudging *this* one up help?

Collect one partial derivative per parameter and you have the
[gradient](/appendix/math/#gradient), which is the object training actually
uses. The curly $\partial$ instead of $d$ is the only notational difference,
and it means exactly "there are other variables and I am ignoring them".
