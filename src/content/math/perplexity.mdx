Perplexity is the exponential of a language model’s average negative log-likelihood when the loss is measured in nats. It can be read as an effective number of choices at each token.

Lower perplexity means the model assigned higher probability to the observed tokens. Compare perplexity only when the dataset, tokeniser, and evaluation procedure are the same. Different tokenisers change what counts as a token, so scores may not be comparable across them.
